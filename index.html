🚢 Smart Container Loading — How We Save You Money

As a factory & trading integrated business, we understand shipping better than trading companies. Jewelry SKUs have very different densities:

  • Light goods: Phone chains, bracelets, anklets — fill volume fast but add little weight
  • Medium: Necklaces
  • Heavy per volume: Rings — small and dense, use up weight allowance quickly
  • Result: We combine categories so your container hits BOTH max weight AND max volume — save up to 30% shipping!
Light: Phone chain / Bracelet Heavy: Ring / Necklace
